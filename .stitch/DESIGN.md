## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the contemplative spirit of traditional Chinese ink wash painting (*Shuimo*, 水墨) and the tactile warmth of handcrafted raw Xuan paper (*Xuanzhi*, 宣纸). Designed for a mindful personal diary, it rejects the sterile, high-frequency ergonomics of modern productivity software in favor of an atmosphere that invites stillness, introspection, and quiet reflection.

The aesthetic philosophy draws from classical East Asian literati traditions: generous compositional negative space (*liubai*, 留白), organic ink gradations (*wucai*, 五彩墨色), and the decisive focal punctum of a vermilion seal (*yinzhang*, 印章). Every interaction feels like setting a loaded calligraphy brush to fibrous paper—deliberate, permanent, yet soft and forgiving.

The audience consists of journalers, poets, writers, and individuals seeking refuge from digital overstimulation. The interface recedes entirely behind the act of personal reflection, offering an editorial experience reminiscent of bound bamboo scrolls, rice paper leaf books, and ink stone desks.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around raw Xuan paper tones, four gradations of mineral carbon ink, and the historic mineral accent of cinnabar vermilion.

- **Canvas & Paper Foundations**:
  - Base Ground (`#F5F2EB`): The primary unbleached paper surface.
  - Pure Ground (`#FAF7F0`): Elevated paper leaves, cards, and modal sheets.
  - Aged Fiber Surface (`#EFE9DC`): Layered secondary zones, recessed input fields, and tool trays.
  - Bamboo/Tea Tone (`#D8D1C3`): Hairline dividers, subtle framing borders, and card perimeters.

- **Ink Spectrum (Typography & Iconography)**:
  - Deep Ink (*Jiaomo*, 焦墨) (`#1E1E1E`): Primary editorial headlines, titles, active glyphs.
  - Concentrated Ink (*Nongmo*, 浓墨) (`#2D2D2D`): Core body copy, timestamps, prominent metadata.
  - Diluted Ink Wash (*Danmo*, 淡墨) (`#68645E`): Secondary captions, passive timestamps, unselected tabs.
  - Faint Water Tint (*Qingmo*, 清墨) (`#9E998F`): Placeholder text, ghost indicators, structural rules.

- **Cinnabar & Vermilion (The Seal Accent)**:
  - Cinnabar Vermilion (`#B83B2E`): Used strictly for focal signatures—confirmations, completed entry markers, bookmark stamps, and primary emotional anchors.
  - Soft Vermilion Wash (`#F3E5E3`): Subtle wash behind seal badges, mood tags, and selected date chips.

Avoid artificial neon accents, synthetic high-saturation primaries, and pure digital pitch black (`#000000`). All dark values must carry the warm, mineral warmth of pine soot ink.

## Typography

Typography delivers a contemplative literary cadence. Headings employ **Noto Serif** to mirror classical Songti and Kaiti brush carving styles, while body copy relies on **Literata** to provide effortless long-form reading with generous leading.

- **Leading & Rhythm**: Line heights are set intentionally loose (`1.7x` to `1.85x` for body text), simulating the spaced vertical columns of traditional calligraphic leaves. This deliberate breathing room prevents visual clutter and invites slower, meditative reading.
- **Letter Spacing**: Extended letter-spacing on labels and section titles (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) introduces an editorial, curatorial presence.
- **Vertical Alignment**: In poetic moments (such as daily aphorisms or solitary verse displays), typography may adopt right-to-left vertical column reading modes where appropriate, framed by expansive whitespace.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on *liubai* (留白)—purposeful emptiness that gives weight to the present entry. The app rejects crammed dashboard metrics and modular overload.

- **Grid Model**: A dedicated single-column narrative flow on mobile devices, constrained within a maximum readable content width of 640px when rendered on larger viewports. A 4-column dynamic grid is utilized for calendar grids and seasonal memory mosaics.
- **Safe Margins**: A wide 1.5rem (`24px`) lateral margin isolates the diary text from edge hardware distractions, reinforcing the sensation of writing onto a clean physical card.
- **Vertical Cadence**: Large section gaps (`2.5rem`) separate distinct diary entries, date boundaries, and reflective prompts. Content transitions are gradual, utilizing atmospheric breathing zones rather than abrupt divider lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use mechanical drop-shadows or saturated neon highlights. Depth is achieved via **Tonal Paper Stacking** and **Water Ink Diffusion**:

1. **Tonal Paper Layering**:
   - Level 0 (Ground Canvas): Textured raw paper background (`#F5F2EB`).
   - Level 1 (Resting Slip/Card): Crisp Xuan leaf sheet (`#FAF7F0`) resting atop the ground.
   - Level 2 (Floating Action Sheets & Modals): Elevated parchment sheet with a double perimeter: a delicate 1px border (`#D8D1C3`) complemented by an ambient, highly-diffused ink wash aura (`box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.05)`).
2. **Ink Diffusion Edges**:
   - Floating elements evoke water drops soaking outward into fibrous paper. Borders are softened with microscopic feathered transitions or hairline framing (`1px solid #E5DFD3`) rather than rigid industrial vectors.
3. **Misty Vignettes**:
   - Scrollable boundaries fade softly into the ground using directional multi-stop gradients from `#F5F2EB` to `transparent`, mimicking morning mountain mist dissolving over water.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts soft, organic geometry (level `1`), emulating hand-sliced paper sheets, dried bamboo tags, and stone seals:

- **Paper Slips and Cards**: Set to subtle 0.25rem (`4px`) or 0.5rem (`8px`) radii, conveying the tactile sensation of deckle-edged or hand-cut Xuan paper leaves.
- **Seals & Stamps**: Strict square or soft circular forms specifically reserved for vermilion accent badges and mood glyphs.
- **Buttons & Chips**: Gentle, non-pill oblongs with 0.25rem corner curves to preserve a composed, literary stillness. Rigid 90-degree corners are reserved strictly for calligraphic frame lines and editorial dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Calligraphy Ink Button)**: Solid deep ink `#1E1E1E` background with unbleached paper `#FAF7F0` text. Soft 4px radius. Micro-interaction: pressing produces a gentle ink-settling opacity dip to `0.85` rather than a standard elevation lift.
- **Secondary Action (Paper Slip Button)**: Background of `#FAF7F0` with a 1px `#D8D1C3` hairline border and `#2D2D2D` text.
- **Seal Stamp Action (Special/Publish)**: Square or round stamp button finished in `#B83B2E` cinnabar ink with white calligraphic relief text or iconography. Framed by a faint double-stroke border resembling carved soapstone.

### Cards & Diary Slips
- Rendered on pure Xuan `#FAF7F0` with a fine 1px border of `#E5DFD3`.
- Internal padding is generous (`space-lg`), honoring the reading rhythm.
- Header contains an editorial date stamp (e.g., *“Rain Water · Second Day”* / *“雨水 · 初二”*) styled in `label-md` using diluted ink `#68645E`.

### Chips & Tags
- Used for seasonal tags (*Jieqi*, 节气), weather, and emotional resonance.
- Styled in soft tea wash `#EFE9DC` with `#68645E` typography and no harsh outline.
- Active or marked state shifts to cinnabar wash `#F3E5E3` with `#B83B2E` text and an optional microscopic square seal indicator.

### Input Fields & Writing Canvas
- Distraction-free, borderless entry canvases.
- Multi-line text areas sit flush on the Xuan ground without rigid input boxes.
- Focus states are indicated by an understated ink-line transition at the bottom edge (`#D8D1C3` transitioning to `#2D2D2D` with ease-out timing).
- Placeholder text is rendered in faint water wash `#9E998F`, fading seamlessly as the first brush stroke is applied.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Markers
- Custom square cinnabar stamp indicators.
- Unchecked: 1px outline in `#D8D1C3` on an aged ground.
- Checked: Inlaid solid vermilion `#B83B2E` square containing an organic calligraphic check or seal character (*“印”* or a single brush dot).

### Lists & Dividers
- List items are separated by generous vertical padding (`space-md`) and an organic hairline divider (`rgba(216, 209, 195, 0.45)`).
- Terminal entries conclude with a centered three-dot ink splash or a small cinnabar glyph marking entry completion.
